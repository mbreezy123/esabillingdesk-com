The problem

Paperwork that steals teaching time

Whether you're on Texas TEFA (Odyssey) or Arizona ESA (ClassWallet), the chores look familiar: submit, fix kickbacks, chase what's stuck, and keep a clear status list.

01

Orders sit unfinished

A stuck or incomplete submission is money that hasn't moved. Someone has to notice and follow up.

02

Kickbacks come back

An invoice that looked fine can return asking for more detail. Fixing and resubmitting is easy to drop.

03

Status lives everywhere

Portal tabs, family emails, and your own notes rarely match. You need one short weekly picture.

FAQ

Straight answers

Do you need my login?

No passwords shared by email. We set up access the safe way the program allows.

Which programs do you cover?

Texas TEFA via Odyssey, and Arizona ESA via ClassWallet.

What if I only have a few students?

The Small School plan is built for that — up to 5 ESA students at $79/month, with the first month free.

Is the first month really free?

Yes. No card needed to start. Email me an invoice and we'll try a month together.

Who are you?

I'm Matt Breen. ESA Billing Desk is a small, independent service I run myself from New York. You deal with me directly.

Do you contact my customers?

No. I don't contact families. Anything that needs a family or the program is flagged for you, with the next step written out.

How do I cancel or opt out?

No contract — cancel anytime by email. Reply "no" to stop outreach, or ask me to delete what you've sent at matt@esabillingdesk.com.
